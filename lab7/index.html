Temporal Network Questions and Findings

  1. How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days?

    Overall connectivity increases, although it fluctuates from day to day. The network begins with three active links and ends with nine, with several late-period peaks of ten links. As the number of links rises, more companies also become active. Cross-region links increase from zero on Day 1 and frequently reach five to seven later in the sequence.

  2. Which companies become more central or commercially active over time?

    The animation does not show one company with fixed centrality because node size and connectivity change each day. Lumen Distribution and Cedar Retail remain active across much of the sequence. Fusion Electronics becomes more commercially active over time, as shown by its larger node on higher-volume days, and Ion Systems shows a similar increase during the later period.

  3. Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters?

    Yes. On Days 1 and 7, the network consists of three disconnected pairs: Ion Systems with Lumen Distribution, Cedar Retail with Evergreen Foods, and Granite Wholesale with Harbor Shipping. Day 4 also remains fragmented, but its seven links form four separate components rather than three pairs. These frames show that the network can split into small clusters even while other days contain more connected structures.

  4. Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time?

    Most relationships are intermittent, so their links repeatedly appear and disappear as the slider moves. Ion Systems–Lumen Distribution, Cedar Retail–Evergreen Foods, Fusion Electronics–Delta Components, and Ion Systems–Juniper Stores are among the more persistent pairs, but none remains active every day. Line width shows that transaction value also changes when links reappear. Fusion Electronics–Delta Components is often wider later in the sequence, while Ion Systems–Juniper Stores shows the clearest increase from narrower early links to wider late links.

  5. Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time?

    The network becomes more cross-regional overall. Day 1 contains only within-region pairs, while cross-region links appear on Day 2 and continue throughout the sequence. The amount still fluctuates, but later frames commonly show five to seven cross-region links. By Day 60, six of the nine active links connect companies from different regions.
